# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors, friends, potential collaborators, and returning users who want to
understand Viper's work and open a hosted project or private system quickly.

## Product Purpose

`viperisuseful.cc` is the central launcher for Viper's public projects and
login-protected infrastructure. Success means visitors can understand the
body of work at a glance, distinguish public products from private systems,
and reach the correct destination without hunting through unrelated pages.

## Brand Personality

Capable, curious, kinetic. The site should feel like a personal operating
system built by one active developer, not a generic agency portfolio or SaaS
landing page.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS card grids and interchangeable startup copy.
- AI-purple gradients, decorative glows, and default glassmorphism.
- Fake terminals, fake dashboards, custom cursors, and ornamental status dots.
- Oversized editorial serif headlines, magazine grids, and repeated mono labels.
- Interfaces that hide destinations behind animation or sacrifice readability.

## Design Principles

1. Make every destination immediately understandable and reachable.
2. Show real project surfaces instead of inventing decorative product previews.
3. Separate public products from private infrastructure without treating either
   as filler.
4. Use motion to explain hierarchy and interaction, never to delay navigation.
5. Keep Viper's personality visible through direct copy and unexpected but
   usable composition.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction requirements. Support keyboard
navigation, visible focus, semantic landmarks, touch targets of at least 44px,
system light and dark modes, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Content and core
navigation must remain complete when JavaScript or animation is unavailable.
