Privacy policy · Last updated 7 June 2026

Privacy

Short version: if you sign up for my newsletter, I collect your email so I can send it to you. I don't sell it, and you can leave whenever you want. The longer version is below.

What I collect

Two things. If you sign up for the newsletter, I collect your email address (and your name, if the form asks for it). The site also counts page views, so I have a rough sense of what people actually read. That tracking runs through Omnisend, the same service that sends the newsletter, and it may set a cookie or use your browser's storage to do it.

Who handles it

The newsletter and the page-view tracking both run through Omnisend, an email marketing service. Your email sits on their servers, not mine, and they handle it on my behalf. Their own privacy policy covers how they store and protect it.

Why I collect it

To send you the newsletter. Updates on what I'm building, the odd release, the occasional rabbit hole. I'm not building a profile on you, and I'm not feeding your data anywhere else.

What I won't do

I won't sell your email. I won't pass it to advertisers. And I won't sign you up for anything you didn't ask for. The only place it goes is Omnisend, so I can send you the thing you signed up for.

Your choices

Every newsletter email has an unsubscribe link, and it works. One click and you're off the list for good. If you'd rather I wipe your data entirely, email me and I'll delete you from Omnisend.

Contact

Questions about any of this, or want your data gone? Email me at viper@viperisuseful.cc.

Changes

If I change how this works, I'll update this page and the date at the top.
